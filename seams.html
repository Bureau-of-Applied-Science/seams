    How this is scored

    Each known failure pattern between two kinds of tool carries a severity from 1 to 3. The severities are judgment from operating real stacks, not measurements. Argue with them.

    Two tools from the same vendor suite are scored one step lower, because the vendor builds and maintains that connection. A pair already at the lowest severity drops out. Password, AI and automation tools are not discounted, since their risk is access and oversight, not the integration.

    The total is multiplied by who finds out first: a customer counts most, an alert and a runbook least. Automation glue adds 15 percent. The score climbs quickly at first and flattens toward 100, so a bigger stack scores higher without ever passing it.

    Possible seams is every pair among your tools, n times n minus 1, divided by 2. Named failure points are the ones this tool has a pattern for.
